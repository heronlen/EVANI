# EVANI Home Charging Calculator — Tariff Changelog

All notable changes to the electricity tariff data, based on Consumer Council NI comparison tables and other supplier sources.

---

## 20 October 2026

**Budget Energy** — A few small moves. The "Billpay 25% Discount" tariff is now a "Billpay 22% Discount": unit rate up from 31.510p to 32.770p and standing charge up from 13.220p to 15.940p. On Economy 7, the Keypad Economy 7 tariff is up from 40.262p/19.641p to 44.087p/21.507p (standing charge now 24.702p). Keypad Loyalty 20% Discount and Keypad Standard 24hr have only tiny adjustments (a fraction of a penny).

All other suppliers (Click Energy, Power NI, SSE Airtricity, Share Energy) — no change since 1 October.

## 1 October 2026

**Share Energy** — Standard and EV/Economy 7 tariffs up from today. Standard "Share 24" rates (Credit and Keypad) rise from 31.960p to 35.99p per unit — standing charges unchanged (13.330p bill pay, 16.670p keypad). EV and Eco 7 customers see day rate up from 32.040p to 36.49p and night rate up from 15.560p to 17.19p — standing charges also unchanged. Consumer Council NI's table hadn't caught up with this change at the time of writing, so these figures are taken directly from Share Energy's own published rates.

**Budget Energy** — Table reshuffled. The "Bill Pay 29% Discount" tariff (which had been the cheapest standard rate around) has been withdrawn, along with the "Keypad 20% Discount" and "£80 Discount and Keypad 20% Discount" tariffs. The remaining standard tariffs (Billpay 25% Discount, Keypad Loyalty 20% Discount, £100 Loyalty 18% Discount, Keypad £60 Loyalty 16% Discount) are unchanged, but the two plain "no discount" tariffs are both up: Keypad Standard 24hr rises from 37.597p to 41.175p, and Bill Pay Standard 24hr rises from 38.367p to 42.010p. On the Economy 7 side, the "Keypad Promotional 1" tariff is up from 33.821p/16.499p to 37.030p/18.070p (standing charge also up, to 24.700p), and it's now a variable contract rather than a 1-year fixed term. Budget's other Economy 7 tariffs are unchanged.

All other suppliers (Click Energy, Power NI, SSE Airtricity) — no change since the last update.

*Note: as ever, we check Consumer Council NI's tables by hand and cross-check against supplier sources when something looks off or out of date — this update needed both, since CCNI hadn't picked up Share Energy's change yet.*

## 4 August 2026

**Budget Energy** — Major refresh, effective from today. New "Bill Pay 29% Discount" tariff added (29.830p — now the cheapest standard rate on the market, new customers only). New "Keypad Loyalty 20% Discount" and "£80 Discount and Keypad 20% Discount" tariffs added. Existing discount tariffs (Billpay 25%, £100 Loyalty 18%, Keypad £60 Loyalty 16%) all repriced upward, reflecting a general Budget Energy increase alongside the new offers.

**SSE Airtricity** — A further reprice on top of the 1 August change already captured. SmartSaver and Keypad standard-rate tariffs, and the Economy 7 SmartSaver/Keypad range, are all up again. The "Keypad Standard Rate 24hr" Economy 7 tariff's standing charge is now correctly showing 10.850p (it was oddly listed as 0.000p in the previous update — this appears to have been a Consumer Council data correction, not a supplier change).

All other suppliers (Power NI, Click Energy, Share Energy) — no change since the last update.

*Note: Budget Energy's Economy 7 "Keypad Promotional 1" tariff is still flagged by Consumer Council NI as having a change scheduled for today, but no new figures have been published yet — we'll pick this up next time it appears.*

## 3 August 2026

**SSE Airtricity** — Rates repriced across the board from 1 August 2026. Fixed-term discount tariffs and Smartsaver/Standard tariffs both moved up; the standing charge on fixed-term Economy 7 tariffs rose to 10.850p. Several new tariffs added, reflecting the fuller range now shown by Consumer Council NI.

All other suppliers (Power NI, Click Energy, Share Energy) — no change from the previous update.

*Note: this changelog was only added to the site on 3 August. The entry below summarises everything that changed since the calculator's last confirmed update in April, since several tariff changes over that period weren't previously logged.*

## Since April 2026 (summary)

**Power NI** — 6.2% increase applied across all standard and Economy 7 tariffs, effective 1 July 2026, including revised Economy 7 standing charges.

**Budget Energy** — Added "Billpay 25% Discount" (cheapest standard rate at the time). Removed the old "Bill Pay Discount 2%" tariffs. Corrected the Keypad Economy 7 night rate.

**SSE Airtricity** — Multiple fixed-term discount tariffs repriced; several new tariffs added on both standard and Economy 7 tables.

**Click Energy** — Withdrawn tariffs: Round the Clock, Twilight, Day & Night, Powershift (all variants), and both Economy 7 Saver tariffs, following a statement citing wholesale gas price rises linked to Middle East tensions. Only Bill Pay 24h, Keypad 24h, Bill Pay Economy 7 and Keypad Economy 7 remain.

**Share Energy** — No rate changes during this period.

*Source: Consumer Council NI electricity and Economy 7 price comparison tables, cross-checked against individual supplier tariff pages when needed. Rates include VAT at 5%.*
